## About
**Mukbang Mania** is an educational puzzle game that makes adding fractions fun for elementary school students (grades 3-4). A hungry streamer goes live and asks for a snack, and the player has to prepare the exact portion: look at the fractions on the plates, cut the food so every piece is the same size, then drag the right number of pieces onto the main plate. Get it right and the streamer eats happily, get it wrong and they end up with a stomachache. Built to be played in the browser on a phone in landscape mode.

## Key Features
**Learn Fractions by Doing**: Fractions are never just numbers. Each plate holds real slices of food, and the player compares sizes, cuts pieces, and combines portions. Adding fractions with different denominators becomes "make the pieces the same size first", with no need to mention LCM or GCF.
 
**Hands-on Drag and Drop**: Pick up a slice with a finger or mouse and release it above the main plate. The slice falls with simple gravity and a small bounce. Slices of different sizes are rejected and return to their plate, so the rule "only equal pieces can be combined" is felt, not memorized.
 
**Preview Before You Cut**: The slice panel shows the current food and, under every cutting option, a picture of the result with its new fraction (for example `8/12`). Kids can compare options and match the number of pieces on both plates before they commit. Each plate can only be cut once, so choices matter.
 
**Explanatory Feedback**: After pressing check, the character reacts first (happy or full), then a result panel pops up. A wrong answer says whether the portion was too small or too big and shows the correct answer, so a mistake becomes a hint instead of a punishment.
